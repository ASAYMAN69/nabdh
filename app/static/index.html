    How this works

    Nabdh puts a screening list in order. It does not diagnose.

    • You upload a list. One row per person. At minimum, age and sex.
    • We put people in order. The order uses age, body measurements, blood pressure, family history, and a few other health questions. A higher score means “screen this person sooner”, not “this person is sick”.
    • We check uploaded results. If the file includes lab results or vital signs, we compare them with published guideline thresholds and flag anything urgent.
    • Children and pregnancy use separate rules. Children follow growth and Well-Child rules. Pregnant women follow the obstetric pathway. They are not given an adult score.

    Small groups are hidden to protect privacy. Outreach previews are simulated; no message is sent. Every action requires clinician sign-off.
